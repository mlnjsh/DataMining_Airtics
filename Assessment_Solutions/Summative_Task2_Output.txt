 STEP 7 : SUMMARY OF FINDINGS
=============================================

1. Fertilizer has a significant effect on yield (p < 0.001).
   Mean yields : F1 = 176.76  F2 = 176.93  F3 = 177.36 
2. Planting density has a significant effect (p < 0.001).
   Mean yields : Low = 176.78  High = 177.25 
3. There is NO significant interaction : the best fertilizer
   is the best at both densities.
4. Tukey HSD : fertilizer 3 beats fertilizers 1 and 2;
   fertilizers 1 and 2 are not significantly different.
5. Assumptions hold (normal residuals, equal variances),
   so the model is valid.
6. Recommendation : use fertilizer 3 at high planting density
   for the maximum yield.
